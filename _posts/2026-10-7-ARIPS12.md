A new version of ARIPS is taking shape: ARIPS 12!

The previous platform was over a decade old and originated from my bachelor thesis. While it has been massively modified and improved
over the years, it suffered some minor and major issues which could not be overcomed without a ground-up remake.


ARIPS 12 is designed to address following features and improvements:
- Keep the general turtlebot style with differential drive, a caster wheel and a tiltable Kinect. It has proven itself over the years
- Keep the two base plates. The laptop goes on top, the lidar and some
components in the middle and the motors and battery below.
- Keep wood as base plate material. Its easy to work on and to modify.
- Keep the Feetech servo ecosystem, since they are precise, affordable, reliable and easy to control.
- Space for a biggger laptop (15" screen) with a dedicated GPU.
- Fast, tool-less removal of top plate without screws.
- Thicker base plate, such that screws can go directly into it without requiring nuts on the other side.
- Sturdier chassis in general.
- Backlash-free Kinect mount.
- Most important: A bigger arm which is physically capable of picking up objects on a table *and* which
can open doors in both directions.

Especially the last point I found impossible to solve with the old chassis. [Pulling on doors was possible in theory]({% post_url 2021-3-23-Proof-of-Concept-Door-Opening %}) (though not very reliable), but pushing from the other side was absolutely impossible
since the robot cannot approch the handle close enough without colliding with the door frame. But without that,
my long-desired goal of full unconstrained navigation through the whole appartement with closed doors would be unfulfillable.

## SCARA Arm
To reach a convential door handle but also be able to pick up objects from the ground, the arm needs a large vertical range of motion.
I desiced to go with a SCARA arm, driven by a lead screw up and down the V-Slot profile.
To drive the lead screw, I use a geared DC motor instead of a conventional stepper motor. My hope is that it will be way faster than a stepper motor,
since I don't want to wait for several minutes for the full range of motion. The DC motor has a rotatory encoder and end-switches for the top and down positions. The STM32 to control the motor is programmed to imitate a Feetech STS3215 servo. This way the linear joint should be easily integratable
into existing ROS drivers and the [LeRobot project](https://huggingface.co/lerobot).

Currently the parts are still under construction. A detailed explanation will follow.

## Transitioning to ROS2
Since ROS1 Noetic went EOL 2025 and all newer software and drivers are written for ROS2, I dared to take the step to completely transition to ROS2. 
There are some 3rd-party libraries and packages which only exist for ROS1, but thanks to the AI it's easier than ever to port them.
Also this is a chance to rework my own software stack, get rid of stale code and design a better overall architecture.

I completely abandoned my own navigation stack and am now using Nav2. Using behavior trees, my special implementations for topological route planning and executions can be now modeled as custom behavior action plugins. This provides a clear separation between individual components, while the old arips_navigation was a monolith running in a single process. 

### Demo of topological navigation
Here is a demo of the topoligical navigation on ARIPS 12. 
For the topological route planning, I have a semantic map server with annotated door positions. A custom route planner will segment the rooms, create 
a route graph with doors as edges, and plan a topological route upon reqest. The route is then executed piece by piece by a behavior tree.
For navigating on flat ground inside a room, I use the usual Nav2 `ComputePathToPose`/`FollowPath` combination. For crossing doors, I implemented a custom `CrossDoorStep` action. Crossing doors requires slow movement strictly perpendicular to the door edge. Otherwise one of the wheels might be slip on the edge which can be several mm high in my appartement. Later on, the bevahior tree will be extended to handle closed doors and other hazards.
